Our Story

A Taste of
Hong Kong

Run by the passionate chef and owner Johnny Tam, The Seafood Guy by 1998 Cafe brings a true slice of Hong Kong's vibrant food culture to the heart of Nottingham.

Walk through our quirky Asian provisions shop at the front and discover a cozy, bustling 40-seater dining space that transports you straight to the streets of Hong Kong. Every dish is prepared with genuine care, authenticity, and flavor.
